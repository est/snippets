Local Cat Discovers Sunbeam

Experts say it might be the warmest spot in the house.

In a stunning turn of events, a local tabby named Whiskers has located a pristine sunbeam on the living room rug. "It was roughly 2:00 PM," witnesses report. "The angle of the sun hit the Persian rug just right."

Whiskers, usually known for sleeping on high shelves, reportedly descended rapidly to claim the spot before the dog could notice. Experts suggest this sunbeam will remain viable for approximately 45 minutes before shifting towards the hallway.

The household remains on high alert. If the sunbeam moves, Whiskers is expected to migrate, potentially disrupting foot traffic in the kitchen area. Residents are advised to step carefully. Furthermore, studies have shown that the optimal nap duration in such a sunbeam exceeds three hours.
