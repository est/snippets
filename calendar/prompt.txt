- all-in-one html, no external deps. No react nor tailwind.

- no breaks between months, days should be displayed continuously, NO separation between months

- Distinguish month by even-odd background color. The color applies from the first day of the month to the last, like a snake. remember Choose your html element wisely.
- infinite scroll
